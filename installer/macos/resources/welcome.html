Welcome to MediaLake

MediaLake searches your files by what they contain: text, code, PDF and Office documents, images, audio and (experimental) video. Open it from the menu bar or with a global shortcut and describe what you are looking for.

An AI model runs on this Mac. It is downloaded once, the first time you start MediaLake, and your files never leave the computer unless you choose to use a remote server.

MediaLake can also keep folders in sync between your computers. A synced folder appears under ~/MediaLake, lists everything at once and loads a file when you open it. Nothing extra is installed for this: no kernel extension and no third-party driver.
